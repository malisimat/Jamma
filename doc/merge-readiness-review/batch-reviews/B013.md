## Ownership and behavior assessment

No blocker was found.

- `NinjamTempoChange::HasSameProposalIdentity` is the one identity implementation. It compares exactly `IntervalLengthSamps`, `SourceSampleRate`, `GrainSamps`, `Bpi`, and `Bpm` with the pre-existing strict `< 0.01f` tolerance. It deliberately does not compare `IntervalPositionSamps` or `AudioBlockStartSample`.
- Coordinator uses that value-owned identity for pending refresh/replacement, ignored-proposal suppression/replacement, and generation-change handling. Scene calls the same method instead of retaining a manual field comparison. Static search finds no `_SameTempo` implementation and no second manual Scene comparison.
- One proposal is derived before tracker state or diagnostics are mutated. `_MakeProposal` requires a valid nonzero interval, sample rate, BPI, and derived grain. Missing BPI therefore emits no desired state, remote grid, tempo request, prompt, command, proposal diagnostic, accepted observation, or pending state; a later complete observation retains its supplied BPI.
- An unchanged identity refreshes only the pending observation's interval position and audio-block sample. Same-interval changes to source rate, BPI/derived grain, or BPM outside tolerance replace pending state and reprompt. Changed ignored identity clears the ignored value, becomes pending, and reprompts.
- Tempo-request acknowledgment, expiry, retry, generation, disconnect, invalid-timing, delayed-join, and `NoSync` flows remain covered by the complete Coordinator suite and the session/network lifecycle tests. No request-state or complete-desired-state regression was found.

The Observe signature remains compatible, but config is not used for remote proposal construction. `Quantiser.cpp` and its pure local master-length/grain inference are unchanged and deterministic under the named prerequisite and full Quantisation suite.
