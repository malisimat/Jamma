## System understanding

The target system has four operational owners. The NINJAM job/integration side owns physical connection availability, timing-session epoch, validation, request/prompt state, follow-policy selection, and publication of one complete desired remote transport value. `AudioHost` is the only audio-boundary integrator: it compares desired with applied state, mutates Timer geometry, owns the common remote-to-local map, and fans neutral corrections/reset/restore values into the engine. `Timer` owns local master geometry and the distinct monotonic scene coordinate. `Station -> LoopTake -> Loop` owns entity membership, anchors, lengths, wrapped audio/MIDI cursors, and automation origin.

The current code crosses those boundaries at identifiable seams. Scene manufactures command variants at `JammaLib/src/engine/Scene.cpp:254`–`:305` and `:403`–`:482`; AudioHost reconstructs authority from them at `JammaLib/src/audio/AudioHost.cpp:165`–`:330`. The callback obtains unsupported live NJClient timing at `AudioHost.cpp:438`–`:453` through the adapter chain at `NinjamController.cpp:102`–`:104`, `NinjamSession.cpp:502`–`:505`, and `NinjamConnection.cpp:709` onward. AudioHost owns `_syncPhaseMap`, but copies common geometry through Station to every take at `AudioHost.cpp:352`–`:364`, `Station.cpp:764`–`:794`, and `LoopTake.cpp:602`–`:679`. These are replacement dependencies, not independently removable code.

For entity `i`, safe restoration remains:

`mappedSource = C0 + round((remoteScene - remoteOrigin) * localMasterLength / remoteMasterLength)`

`entityPhase[i] = positive_mod(mappedSource - entityAnchor[i], entityLength[i])`

The first value is common; the second is not. A loop of length `2M` or `3M` must not be reduced modulo `M`. Rebase may move only the common map origin; it must not recapture entity anchors. An epoch change clears map, anchors, and every consumer gate before new authority. `NoSync` clears authority without moving any cursor. Continuous and Block follow remain separate policies even though both use the same map model. This is the non-negotiable equivalence criterion for every candidate below, consistent with the protected glossary at `00-scope-and-inventory.md:17`–`:37` and the human mandate at `decisions.md:11`.

Impact dimensions are therefore interpreted as follows:

- **Thread/API impact** identifies who may publish, compare, mutate, or consume; callback work must remain bounded, allocation-free, exception-free, lock-free, and free of I/O.
- **Persistence impact** identifies serialized local configuration only. Live epoch, desired/applied authority, map, anchors, generation, remote phase, and follow policy must never be persisted.
- **UI/VST/MIDI impact** distinguishes direct edits from regression-only consumers. Scene prompts may change; VST2/VST3 are not cleanup-owned but consume `Station` host time derived from `Timer::SceneSamplePos` and `CurrentMusicalPosition` at `Station.cpp:433`–`:448`, then expose it at `Vst2Plugin.cpp:711`–`:719` and `Vst3Plugin.cpp:1767`–`:1779`. MIDI event cursor and automation origin are direct timing consumers and must be asserted separately.
- **Asset impact** is none for every in-scope candidate. Any TGA/shader/resource/project edit is prohibited collateral.
